# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate of contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels. Critique code and ideas, not people. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish private information, or repeatedly disrupt project discussions.

Keep discussions relevant and provide reproducible technical feedback. Respect others' time, acknowledge uncertainty, and do not pressure contributors for unpaid support or disclose security exploits publicly.

Repository maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions, or restrict participation proportionately. Once the repository is published, contact its owner privately where a verified channel is available to report conduct concerns; avoid including private personal information in public issues. No unverified reporting address is provided here.
